Many agents at once, or one agent over time. On the left, under HORIZONTAL, four windows of the same size sit side by side at one moment, each working on its own piece of one graph: red solid dots joined by solid lines are work already written, green hollow rings joined by dashed lines are work not yet written. Where the graph crossed from one window to the next it is cut, an envelope (a message) bridges each gap, and the pieces that lay between the windows fall into a heap marked lost. On the right, under VERTICAL, one window is carried down an axis marked time; faint outlines show where it was earlier. Its red record runs down beside those outlines, outside them, with short branches reaching into each; its newest node joins the green work in the window by a dashed line, and an arrow marked by address reads one old red node back into the window as a copy, joined to the new green work.
