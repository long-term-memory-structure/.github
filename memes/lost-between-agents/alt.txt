Two panels. HORIZONTAL, many agents at once: four context windows stand side by side, each holding a piece of one task's graph, and every one is mid-work at the same moment: the first part of each piece is written, solid red dots on solid lines, and the rest is not yet written, dashed green rings on dashed lines. Envelopes fly back and forth between neighbouring windows, while pieces of the graph fall through the gaps between the windows into heaps marked lost. VERTICAL, one agent over time: the same graph, whole, runs down the page on disk into one context window at its end, and that window reads an early node back by its address: an arrow runs from the node to a copy of it inside the window.
