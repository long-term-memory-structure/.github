Parts built apart do not fit. At the top, under MANY AGENTS AT ONCE, three windows of the same size stand side by side, and in each one an agent builds its own piece of one graph without seeing the other windows, so the three pieces come out in three different styles, red discs, red squares and red diamonds, at three different heights. Where two pieces should join, in the gap between their windows, each agent built its own half of the joint: the two heavy ends reach the middle of the gap from either side, one higher than the other, and do not meet. At the bottom, under ONE AGENT OVER TIME, one window of the same size sits at the growing end of one graph drawn in one style. The part already written, solid red discs on solid lines, runs outside the window to the left; the part not yet written, hollow green rings with dashed outlines on dashed lines, grows inside the window, joined to the red across its frame. A solid arrow marked by address reads one older red node back into the window as a ringed copy, and a dashed line joins the copy to the new green work.
