Before and after. BEFORE: one context window packed wall to wall with a red graph of everything already written, solid discs joined by solid lines; the green of what is not yet written, hollow dashed rings, is crushed into its bottom corner. AFTER: the red graph has moved out of the window onto a disk, where it is kept; the same window now holds the green graph with room to grow, and a solid arrow marked by address copies one older red node from the disk into the window, where a dashed line joins it to the green.
